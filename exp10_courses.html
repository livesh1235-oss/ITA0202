Academic Programs Offered

NIET offers undergraduate (B.E. / B.Tech.), postgraduate (M.E. / M.Tech.), and doctoral (Ph.D.) programs structured under a flexible choice-based credit system (CBCS).

Undergraduate (UG) Degree Programs:

  1. B.E. Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) — 180 Seats
  2. B.Tech. Information Technology (IT) — 120 Seats
  3. B.Tech. Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AI&DS) — 120 Seats
  4. B.E. Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE) — 120 Seats
  5. B.E. Mechanical Engineering (MECH) — 60 Seats
