About the Institute

Established in 1998, the National Institute of Engineering & Technology has stood as a beacon of pioneering research, holistic education, and ethical leadership in technological advancements. Spread across 75 lush green acres, our campus provides an inspiring environment for intellectual growth and innovation.

Our Vision

To be a globally recognized center of excellence in engineering education and research, nurturing innovative leaders with ethical and societal commitment.

Our Mission

  • Deliver dynamic, industry-aligned curricula.
  • Foster research and entrepreneurship.
  • Instill moral values and professional ethics.

Core Values

  • Academic Integrity & Transparency
  • Diversity, Equity, and Inclusivity
  • Sustainability and Social Impact
